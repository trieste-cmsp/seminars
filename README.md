## Trieste CMSP Seminars

This is the website repository for the unified announcement service of the Condensed Matter & Statistical Physics seminars in the Trieste Scientific Area.

### Workflow

1. Submit a [*New Announcement*](https://forms.gle/3XT3qna9dFiixfQR8) using the link-button at the [Trieste-CMSP](http://ts-cmsp.github.io/seminars) website.
2. The announce will be reviewd by the admins.
3. Once accepted an automatic email will be delivered to any interested CMSP scientist in Trieste.



### Scope

This is a free service planned and realized by the admins with the goal of providing maximal diffusion for seminars to the condensed matter and/or statistical physics community in Trieste. We (the admins) recognized that each institution in the Trieste area owns an internal seminar announcement system, which in our opinion is both
i) highly inefficient for the organizer and ii) very dispersive in reaching the target scientific audience.

### Disclaimer

None of the work provided by this service is, in any way, supported by or representing the scientific institutions reached by the service.
