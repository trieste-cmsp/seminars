Second Seminar

Speaker: Adriano Amaricci (CNR IOM)

Date: Tuesday, 03 Feb 2026
Time: 14:00
Location: aula A sissa

Abstract

Non-equilibrium phenomena are ubiquitous in quantum many-body systems. In this talk, I will discuss recent progress on driven quantum spin systems, focusing on relaxation, emergent hydrodynamics, and transport. $E = mc^2$.
