# Abstract

Dear Colleagues,

We are pleased to announce the opening of the call for applications to the 23rd International Workshop on Computational Physics and Materials Science: Total Energy and Force Methods, which will take place at the International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP) in Trieste, Italy, from January 13 to 15, 2027.

Please register through the ICTP platform as indicated on the conference website https://indico.ictp.it/event/11409 .

*The registration deadline is October 11, 2026*.

The ?Total Energy? is a renowned workshop about recent progress in electronic structure methods and their applications. It has traditionally been held in Trieste every two years since the 1980s and has become one of the most popular events of the international ab initio electronic structure community. The goal of the workshop is to provide a broad view of recent developments, current challenges, and future directions of the field.

The program features focused sessions on novel functionals, theory and applications of machine learning, correlated materials and excited states, electron-phonon coupling, materials design and discovery, applications and materials platform for quantum computing, time-dependent and out-of-equilibrium phenomena, and more.
