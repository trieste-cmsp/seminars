# Abstract

Dear colleagues,

It is our pleasure to announce the Workshop 1 of the SISSA Probability Trimester. The workshop will take place next week, 28 September to 2 October. Participation is open to everyone.

The workshop has a broad range of topics, including random matrix theory, stochastic PDEs, quantum field theory, and random tensors.
