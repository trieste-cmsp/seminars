# Abstract

Many statistical models of interacting spins can be reformulated as loop
models through high-temperature expansions. At criticality, these models
are described by a Conformal Field Theory (CFT). While the spectrum of
critical exponents has been known since the late 1980s, the
determination of correlation functions in loop models has seen little
progress until the recent introduction of conformal bootstrap
techniques.

In this talk, I will present our recent derivation of the three-point
functions of conformal loop models. Starting from a transfer-matrix
approach on the lattice, I will explain how the conformal data emerge in
the continuum limit. If time permits, I will also discuss the structure
of the space of states of loop models, and show how it leads to the exact
computation of general correlation functions.
