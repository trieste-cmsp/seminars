# Abstract

The aim of this event is to bring together mathematicians, statistical physicists, condensed matter physicists, and experimentalists interested in the effects of quasiperiodicity and fractality in quantum physics, and to stimulate interactions between these different communities. For more information and to register, please visit:
https://indico.sissa.it/e/quasiperiodic
