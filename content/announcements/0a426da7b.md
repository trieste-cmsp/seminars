# Abstract

Abstract: In these lectures I will review some mathematical problems associated to the definition and construction of quantum field theories. Mainly from the probabilistic viewpoint but also paying some attention to the quantum aspects. The goal is to give a wide perspective on the open problems and opportunities for research, in the era of large language models.
