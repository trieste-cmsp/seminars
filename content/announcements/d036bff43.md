# Abstract

Do we really need to go through the ?energy transition?, i.e. changing the way we produce, distribute and use energy? Are we actually doing it? What are the challenges? What are the technologies that are emerging to sustain this transition? How do they work, and do they really work? What is the role of ?materials? ? from the traditional and non-traditional fuels, to the newly designed materials (and nanomaterials) that enable the energy transition technologies? And what is the role of (critical) raw materials that need to be mined, refined and traded around the globe, in delicate and dangerous geopolitical dance?
