# Abstract

Whether gravity must obey the laws of quantum mechanics
has remained unresolved for nearly a century. Recent self-consistent
models coupling quantum mechanics and classical gravity sharpen the
question, leaving experiment as the ultimate arbiter. Gravity-
mediated entanglement offers a route to test gravity's quantum
nature, though the challenges are formidable. I will discuss our efforts
using milligram-scale torsion pendulums and optical cavities to
approach this regime. I will elaborate on our recent results of
mechanical state control starting to approach to the quantum regime,
and report on the status of two body gravitational coupling
measurements.
